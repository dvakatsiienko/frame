# dima-strategy-branches — the six branch notes (not imported; read on demand)

moved out of `dima-strategy` on dima's word (2026-10-12, delete-first r31); the rules that bind every report stay resident there.

The six branches below replaced the old «all six equally weighted» rule (dima's word, 2026-10-05):
they stay as notes on each area's spirit, the vector decides what comes first.

## 📋 pm — the tracker and its flow

**A chiller loop, not a fuller board.** Dima: «you're meant to optimize the flow, not make it
hotter.» Capture is cheap for an agent, retirement is expensive for him — so the measure is how
much got retired, at how little of his attention. Every other branch produces tickets, so pm is
where the other five become visible. Moves live in [[craft-pm]]; `freebie` is the delegation escape
hatch.

## 🤖 fleet — the agents themselves

**One mind, many surfaces, one source of truth.** The coordinator migration to cc resolved the
old surface-sync mess by deletion ([[sys-boundaries]]). Two roles stay distinct: clio
(planning and the tracker) and codie (a disposable context, the edits).
**Standing value: be an expert of yourself** — every surface knows its own tools, config and
vocabulary cold at session start; asking Dima what a label means is a bug.

## 🔧 dima's tools — git, shell, nvim, dotfiles

**Shared tools where the agent is the majority user**, which inverts who the ergonomics serve.
His reframe: «you use my git ~98% more than me… so you'd be interested in having a proper setup.»
Underneath: agent and human commit under one identity; the `Agent:` trailer tells them apart
(`git who`). Deliberately after the
fleet settles; waiting is not neglect.

## 🛠️ harness — our own coding app

**Wanted, no deadline — planned, not scheduled.** «i'd still prefer a harness that satisfies
my style 😎, and it's interesting.» The harness is **our own agent app**, in the class of
conductor, t3 code or pi (a bare terminal harness) — never the build flow, which is the shift
(FRM-266). The order (dima, 2026-08-30): investigate first, eval the effort, THEN decide
build-or-not; eval a simplified version to try before any real build. It lives in FRM-43, next to
the cli project. Hold the tension out loud: it is the most seductive meta-work on the board.

## 👁️ visibility — seeing what the agents do

«upgrade you even more = visibility» — ambient awareness while work happens, and «let's try an mvp
first». Same root as the tools branch: he cannot tell his work from the agents' after the fact —
visibility and attribution are one question at two layers. Notice when other work quietly builds a
piece of this (sline, session diffs, artifacts); that is the cheap way it gets built.
