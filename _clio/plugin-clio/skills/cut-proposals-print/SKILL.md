# cut-proposals-print — a cut he can judge from the list alone

dima, 2026-11-06: «if you just print me a cut proposal with ticks in orbit, I do not understand what to cut and why you think like that.» the print is the proposal; orbit only carries his tick on it. measure first: this skill shapes verdicts, it never makes them.

## the shape

a bold verdict line with the counts, then one block per item, in build order:

```
**12 chunks: 7 stay, 3 trimmed, 1 merged, 1 cut**

✂️ **cut** · [FRM-468](https://linear.app/x-com/issue/FRM-468) the jump list of compactions
- what it is: a command that shows where a session compacted, and lets you jump there
- why: nobody jumped in 14 days, 0 real reads; the 4 hand reads were looking for a compact
- **you lose: your «looks very useful» on it.** a real compaction mystery reopens it

🪶 **trim** · [FRM-414](https://linear.app/x-com/issue/FRM-414) the review command's wrong messages
- what it is: two messages that tell a coder the wrong next step
- why: the rest of it was never built, and no lane asked
- **you lose: nothing.** the screenshot part moves to the stats board
```

## the verdict words

- ✂️ **cut**: it goes, closed with its reason
- 🪶 **trim**: part of it goes; name the part
- 🧩 **merge**: it joins another ticket, its words with it
- 🚚 **move**: another lane, milestone or order
- 💤 **park**: it stays, nothing runs it
- ✅ **stay**: one line, no block

## the rules

- **what it is** comes first, in words he would use. a newcomer knows the thing from this line
- **why** carries the one number that decides, plus what it counts. a guess says «my guess»; no number says «not counted»
- **you lose** names what of his it undoes, his own words where they exist. nothing lost says «nothing»
- three lines an item, one sentence each. a ✅ is one line
- no file, function, flag, rule or internal label. the real thing's plain name only; a ticket id only as its link
- at most 5 blocks a print, then a pause. a long queue goes in batches, or as a page with the verdict here

## the failure this replaces

his tick list read «FRM-414 cut: board screenshot, 0 uses in 14 days.» he had to ask: what is the board screenshot? maybe it is not used because it is not wired? it was never built, so 0 uses said nothing. a cut that makes him ask has failed, however true its number.

so before a «0 uses» reaches him, say which it is: built and unused, built and unwired, or never built.

## done

every item has its block, and each block passes: a tired reader knows what it is, why it goes, and what he loses, with no question left. then the orbit ask, «as printed», names the verdicts in its pick.
