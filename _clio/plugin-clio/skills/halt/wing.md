# a wing's halt — this wing closes, main and the other wings go on

it starts on dima's «halt» in this window, or on main's message when she halts (PK-351:W41, W77). the CST, the gazette, the flawlog flush, the inbox and stopping crow or helga are main's; a wing runs none of them.

run in order, each step done before the next:

1. **tails**: finish what is yours, and start no big ticket. a lane you run: land what is ready and stop its members, or name where it stands for step 8
2. **flyway** (`flyway: wing <N>`): finished lines into the done note (`node script/flyway-done.ts <PK> <n>`), the rest groomed and sorted (`--sort`) and reorganized to the real state. it stays open, for the next wing of your number
3. **pocket**: `backlog task list --labels clio-wing-<N> -s open --plain`; each item marked done or handed back, label off; the pocket hook commits each write
4. **tickets**: `x linear list --team FRM --label clio-wing-<N>`; close the related ones that landed (`clio:pm`), name why the rest stay, label off
5. **cleanups**: no trash, no stray, no tail: your scratch trashed, every background shell and agent stopped, and each live `Monitor` stopped by its id with `TaskStop`; a live watch makes a wing wait instead of halt
6. **sync**: commit, then `~/frame/_clio/.claude/hooks/tree-sync.sh land`. your tree stays (W47)
7. **siesta**: one due or opened runs now (`clio:siesta`), so the close ends on no nudge
8. **hand over**: one `SendMessage` to «🦉 clio», the turn's last act. your prompts reach no prompt log, so it opens with «dima's asks here»: every ask or wish of his from this thread, his words copied, each with its home (the ticket or pocket id you wrote, or «main's call»). then your progress, what's done, what's left, every external write, and each watch on a lane you stopped in step 5 (what it waited for), so main re-arms it; a file too long for it sits in your tree's `.scratch/`, its full path in the message. if main told you she is second-winding, send it after her back line
9. **stop**: end the turn. main archives you; dima's or her word, never your own

**done when**: `node ~/frame/_clio/script/clio-lanes-radar.ts` shows your tree level with main and clean, the pocket list of step 3 holds only your flyway, and the message of step 8 is sent.
