# pm — closing

read before any close, cancel, merge or reshape proposal (`clio:pm` § write and close).

## closing — proposed, except the four auto-closes

auto-close is for four named cases, each said out loud: **stale** (the body proves it), **dupe**
(the surviving id named), **freebie done in place** (shipped this turn), **landed** (merged, its done
test run live, a new-surface ticket's `🔌 fleet wired:` caller seen making one real call (`x stats`, a transcript; a rare-event caller's exception on dima's word; no call yet → In Progress behind a blocked-by or `needs human`, or Todo with a dated `⏰ due:`), and the close test below passed: closed at its land, a pr lane's merge turn or a quick lane's clean, named in the reply; an open ❓ or an unmet W-line moves it to Todo with the ↩️ line, its asks printed; a failed look does the same). everything else goes to a printed «closable, because …» list and waits for his word, two closes a turn, each with
what it was and why it can close; the halt lists them all at once.

- **when**: `craft-fleet-flow` stage 8; a test that needs dima's hands is an orbit ask that turn, never a wait he has to discover
- each entry passes the close test: what became better (the closing word), what strays (findings,
  sub-items, dima notes the close would orphan), each wish W-line met, moved (FRM-N) or open → Todo; its milestone's count read; a milestone's last close runs the kill side, `x stats --days 30 | jq .data.unused`, each 0-call verb revived or put to dima
- 🙋‍♂️ **the ask-guard, above every skill template**: a close, cancel, merge or reshape of a ticket
  carrying dima's asks prints them first, one line each with its destination (answered here, moves
  to X, dies); the ticket cannot close until he verdicts each — «yes let's do», «not important anymore», or a steer; no answer, not resolved
- his «close all listed» closes the batch; silence closes nothing; an approved close gets its closing word
- a lane's ticket goes Done through `x lane landed`, the hand `--state Done` after it
- a closed ticket's pocket item died at its birth (`habit-shared-files` § the pocket); one still open is set done with a `close:` line
