# low-tide-cuts — what stays, what goes, and a print he can judge alone

dima, PK-367:W2 (2026-11-09 inbox, dima): why: we started a practice of cuts, since we spotted that we plan redundant stuff; i don't want us to build dead weight, so a cut process should be established. measure first: the filter test makes the verdict, the print shapes it.

## when it runs

- **every low tide** (`clio:low-tide`), over the planned tickets of the next big step, before its first chunk is signed
- **every milestone close**, over what the milestone built: the kill side of `craft-pm` § the freebie rule
- **his «what can we cut?»**, any time, over the set he names

## the filter test — an item stays only if it passes

- **planned work** re-passes ⚖️ worth it against today's door, never the door it was signed against (root invariant 3):
  - it still removes something real: a pain, a hand step, a repeat ask of his
  - no cheaper door exists now: a landed tool, skill or plan (herdr's, a sibling ticket) that already does it
  - its upkeep is named, and smaller than what it removes
- **built work** passes the same, plus one real use in 30 days:
  - a use is a run that ran: posted output, a read, a call in a log; never a launch, a scheduled slot or silence
  - say which it is before a «0 uses»: built and unused, built and unwired, or never built
  - a thing rare by design (a halt step, a recovery door, a safety net) is weighed on worth it alone once dima's word exempts it as a rare event; without his word it prints as 💤 park with «rare by design?» in its why, and keeps running as now until his word
  - a built thing with 0 uses gets one revive try first (wire it to a caller), and one with no counter prints «unmeasured», never «kept» (`craft-pm` § the freebie rule)
- a fail is ✂️ cut or 🪶 trim, a doubt 💤 park, never a quiet keep; a pass is ✅ stay. his own ask on an item fails only to him: it prints with «you lose» and waits for his tick

## the print — a cut he can judge from the list alone

dima, 2026-11-06: «if you just print me a cut proposal with ticks in orbit, I do not understand what to cut and why you think like that.» the print is the proposal; orbit only carries his tick on it.

a bold verdict line with the counts, then one block per item, in build order:

```
**12 chunks: 7 stay, 3 trimmed, 1 merged, 1 cut**

✂️ **cut: [FRM-468](https://linear.app/x-com/issue/FRM-468) the jump list of compactions**
- what it is: a command that shows where a session compacted, and lets you jump there
- why: nobody jumped in 14 days, 0 real reads; the 4 hand reads were looking for a compact
- **you lose: your «looks very useful» on it.** a real compaction mystery reopens it
```

the verdict words:

- ✂️ **cut**: it goes, closed with its reason
- 🪶 **trim**: part of it goes; name the part
- 🧩 **merge**: it joins another ticket, its words with it
- 🚚 **move**: another lane, milestone or order
- 💤 **park**: it stays and nothing new is built on it; a doubt park keeps running as now, until his word
- ✅ **stay**: one line, no block

- **what it is** comes first, in words he would use: a newcomer knows the thing from this line
- **why** carries the one number that decides, plus what it counts; a guess says «my guess», no number says «not counted»
- **you lose** names what of his it undoes, his own words where they exist; nothing lost says «nothing»
- three lines an item, one sentence each; no file, function, flag or internal label, a ticket id only as its link
- at most 5 blocks a print, then a pause; a long queue goes in batches

the failure this replaces: his tick list read «FRM-414 cut: board screenshot, 0 uses in 14 days.» he had to ask what the board screenshot was, and whether it was unused because it was unwired. it was never built, so 0 uses said nothing.
