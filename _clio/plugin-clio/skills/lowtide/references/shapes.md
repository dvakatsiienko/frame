## the chunk line

`N. [FRM-N](https://linear.app/x-com/issue/FRM-N) **S** — <what it does>`

- the size bold: XS, S or M; unsized prints `?`
- what it does: one sentence of 10–25 words in his words, clear about what is under the hood; never the ticket title pasted
- the running chunk: 🛼 leads its line, its subject bold, one nested line: `left: <what>, ~N min (estimate)`; the estimate is steps left × today's minutes per finished step, from the lane plan's ticks; no tick today → «not estimated»
- a step with no ticket prints its name in the link's place
- a blank line between lanes
