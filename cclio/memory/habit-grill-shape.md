# grill shape — matt's skill, a title, a pre-filled answer block

dima, 2026-10-09, after I packed a cli 4 grill into the ⏳ block as one wall: «you are either rushing, or choosing the wrong shape».

- **every grill loads `mattpocock-skills:grilling` first** and asks in its round shape (❓ **Qn** - **title**: body, ➡️ recommendation, `---`); never an improvised list
- **a grill opens with its title line**: what we grill, and the ticket it folds into, or the lane's name when it is a lane
- **the round ends with a pre-filled answer fence** under «⏳ waiting on your word:», one line per question — `Qn. <short title> ➡️ <my pick> ⬅️` — so he types only his steer after the arrow; an empty steer means he agrees. open lane asks ride the same fence below the round's lines
- his answers fold into the ticket body the same turn (`decided`), then the next round recomputes the frontier
- **a chunk prep is a full prep, ready to dispatch, never a grill alone** (dima, 2026-10-09: «a grill assumes exit lines too … do not rush»). picked and started, it prints in this order: **CHUNK TITLE** + its body (what it is) → the grill rounds → the exit lines (grepped against main, each naming a real verb or surface) → **seal** (`x brief preflight` passes). «grilled» without sealed exit lines is reported as not ready
- **exit lines are cclio's, filled up front, never his to review** (dima, 2026-10-09: «they are too detailed for me to even review, so I'd leave exit line automation to you»): every grilled chunk gets its exit lines in the same turn its last round lands, written into the ticket and preflighted; the chunk print shows them collapsed to a count («8 exit lines, sealed»), and only a line that carries a decision he never made goes back to him as a grill question
