# recipe-first — a branch or a big research becomes a recipe

dima, 2026-09-29: «whenever you spot some noticeable branch (for example, design or voice), it
must be folded into a recipe. every time we spin up sophisticated research, it has to be folded
into a recipe to avoid creating sophisticated research vectors each time.»

- **the trigger**: a research round with several vectors, or a feature area that will be
  refreshed again (a tool stack, a domain like design or read-aloud). the tell is writing
  research vectors from scratch for something we have researched before.
- **the move, same session**: fold the vectors into `docs/recipes/<name>.md` per `_spec.md` —
  the want in dima's words (quote his asks from the thread), the vectors, the artifacts it keeps
  fresh, the run. the next «refresh X» opens the recipe instead of re-deriving the vectors.
- **every recipe run ends with an overhaul proposal**, never silent edits: what is new, what it
  changes in our setup, noop included.
- the fleet-wide scan for candidates lives on its ticket; this leaf is the habit, not the backlog.
