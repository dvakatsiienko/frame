🐠 catch — cclio's keep-in-mind shelf for dima. dima owns this file; the mod reads it fresh at every session start in cclio's thread, so an edit binds from the next session.

catch holds only what dima would otherwise re-ask. it never holds what another part of the board shows:
- an ask that wants his tick → orbit
- your next moves, a trial's count → phases
- a member's state → sessions; a lane's step → glide; meters → the band

a piece is one line, ≤60 chars, shaped `<subject>: <state>`, its kind emoji first. the why goes in its hidden note.

the seven kinds, each with when to add it and when it leaves; the mod keeps no clock, so you clear a piece the turn its lifetime ends:
- 📍 effort — a multi-session effort with no glide card moves · the next move replaces it, the close clears it
- 🧷 receipt — his ask lands somewhere he can't see (pocket, linear, a spec) · until he clears it, or the item closes
- ✅ unseen — a merge-ready pr, a red, a down watch, a done member, found in a turn he did not start · after his next prompt is answered
- 🎯 taken as — you applied his tick or answer with a reading past its literal words · one turn
- ⏰ remind-me — he says «remind me», «when i'm back», «ask me after» · when it fires it becomes an orbit ask and leaves
- 💡 fact — a finding that changes how he works, or an answer he asked for twice · 24 h, or his clear
- 📦 unshipped — unpushed or uncommitted work meets a spawn, a merge, a checkpoint, or turns a day old · the push or commit clears it

5 pieces show, 7 at most; at 7 the oldest 💡 leaves first, and with no 💡 clear one before adding. when nothing fits a kind, add nothing: an empty catch is normal.
