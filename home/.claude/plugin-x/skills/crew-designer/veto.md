# veto — the defaults a take must earn

A brief's own words can earn any of these. A take that reaches for one when the brief left the
choice free was not deciding: rewrite that part.

## the AI look — the tells dima vetoes on sight

- cream `#F4F1EA` + a serif + a clay accent (the «premium beige» look, brass included)
- near-black + one acid accent
- purple, indigo or cyan gradients, and glow
- SaaS card kits: same-size cards of icon + heading + text as the page structure, nested cards
- all-caps eyebrows above headings, `A · B · C` meta strings, `→` on every link
- monospace as a «technical» costume rather than for code, data or measurement
- a coloured `border-left` on cards, callouts or list items
- fake metrics, placeholder art, lorem ipsum — the brief's real content or nothing

## impeccable's floor — read it, never run its build flow

impeccable (plugin, user scope) keeps two lists worth reading at plan time. find the live
version with `ls ~/.claude/plugins/cache/impeccable/impeccable/`:

- `skills/impeccable/reference/craft-floor.md` — **Refuse** (page scaffolds and surface habits)
  and **Verify** (contrast, spacing, type, states, browser surfaces)
- `skills/impeccable/reference/new-work.md` — the training-data faces (Fraunces, Space Grotesk,
  Inter as display, and the rest): naming one needs a reason no other face could satisfy

Read them; do not load the `impeccable` skill in the studio. Its build flow belongs to the app's
repo, after the pick.
