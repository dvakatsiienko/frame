# herdr — glossary

the words `herdr/FTR.md` and our herdr mods use, each defined once (dima settled the words for a mod, a layout and a block at 18:45).

- **herdr mod** — one folder with a `main.js` that draws one panel in herdr; ours are named `x-herdr-*`; always said with «herdr», never a bare «mod»
- **cc native mod** — a Claude Code mod of plugin `x`; ours are named `x-mod-*`; always said in full
- **herdr layout** — the file that places herdr mods in the window and sets the look (`layouts/*.json`)
- **herdr layout block** — one spot in a herdr layout where a herdr mod sits
- **x-herdr** — our setup inside herdr: its layout and our mods, each mod under its own name; `x-herdr-*` names a mod alone, a part inside a mod is a plain word with its mod named
- **block** — short for herdr layout block; one block holds one mod, and where a part sits is the layout's alone (HRD-0001)
- **thread** — one Claude Code session running in a herdr terminal
- **Chat view** — a thread drawn as a conversation by x-herdr-chat; the view dima lives in
- **Terminal view** — the same thread as its raw terminal, where cc's own band and board draw
- **dock** — the stack under the Chat view's log, inside x-herdr-chat: the band on top, the quill, the ledge under it
- **band** — the dock's top card: the thread's meters (context, weekly use); not cc's own band, the row cc draws in the Terminal view
- **quill** — the dock's text box, where dima writes to a thread
- **ledge** — the dock's bottom card: the thread's status line with its clock and Stop, the buttons, the agent's progress
- **x-herdr key** — a key of ours in herdr's `keys.json`, its action naming an x-herdr mod (`x-herdr-status.quill`); listed in `hotkeys.js`, shown on x-herdr-settings' keys page
- **x-herdr-settings** — «settings» in speech: the one dialog for x-herdr's choices (pages general, dock, chat, claude code mods, keys), opened by the band's 🎛️ or ⌘⇧,; drawn by x-herdr-chat today, not a mod of its own; its values live in `~/.claude/shelf/herdr-settings/settings.json`
- **x-herdr-chat-settings** — «chat settings» in speech: the popover the chat header's gear opens, only how the chat reads (text size, font, width, spacing); his choices there are the **chat look**
- **settings row** — one choice in x-herdr-settings: a key, a title, a one-line why, one control (toggle, checks or stepper) and a default
- **x-herdr-chat** — our chat mod, the Chat view itself, built new in place of zoo's chat (FRM-638)
- **iTrout** — dima's layout, `layouts/itrout.json` under the hood (FRM-633)
- **chat look** — his choices for how the chat reads (text size, font, width, spacing, tool calls, his messages, colours, accent), kept in herdr's shared store under `chat.look`; the chat reads it
- **x-herdr-chat-settings** — the chat settings popover, a herdr mod of its own the header's ⚙ opens; it edits the chat look
- **x-herdr-chat-header** — the whole top row of a thread, in the Chat view and the Terminal view alike: the thread's name and folder, + Files Changes, search, the chat look's ⚙ and the Chat | Terminal switch; one mod placed twice by the layout
- **x-herdr-terminal-header** — the head row of the tray (the bottom quick terminal), part of x-herdr-terminal
- **x-herdr-status** — the status bar along the bottom of the middle column, under the tray: branch, folder, the open-in commands (VS Code), the command palette
- **sidebar** — the window's left column: antenna today; settled to hold glide at its bottom
- **deck** — the window's right column, drawn by x-herdr-deck: orbit on top, then glide, phases pinned at its bottom; no thread list, antenna holds it
- **antenna** — the sidebar's list of every thread, once each, in his order, grouped by space or not, a still dot per row; drawn by `x-herdr-antenna`, its knobs in its ⚙️ popup; it replaced the agents list (FRM-618)
- **orbit** — the asks threads put to dima, in the deck; one ask is **ticked** or **replayed**
- **phases** — each thread's next moves, the lines it keeps with orbit's plan op; one block pinned at the deck's bottom, a group per thread in antenna's order
- **glide** — one card per running lane, its steps and «we are here»; drawn in the deck today, settled at the sidebar's bottom, stuck there and growing upward
- **x-herdr-deck** — our deck mod: orbit, glide and phases; it publishes each thread's facts for antenna and the chat
- **x-herdr-agents** — our old agents list, out of the layout since antenna took the sidebar; kept in the tree until his word
- **x-herdr-tabs** — our tab bar above a space's panes, each agent tab by its session name; copied from zoo's `my-tabs` (FRM-567)
- **draft** — what dima has typed in the quill and not yet sent; kept per thread across a reload
- **mark** — the quill's words marked to join a thread's turn (🍕), kept with the draft by one owner (`quill.js`) until a turn takes them or he takes the mark off
- **press** — dima's tick, replay or untick on an ask, one record each in the deck (`createPresses`) until the thread's shelf shows it
- **ask** — an orbit ask a thread put on his deck (and on cc's own board); a **tick** (🛎️) accepts it, a **replay** (🔁) sends it back with his steer
- **look card** — an ask opening «look:» (a 👀 before it allowed), a thread's request that dima looks at a finished change
- **test card** — 🧪, an ask opening «test:» (a 🧪 before it allowed); «try …» alone is a plain ask; a thread's request that dima tries what it cannot test itself
- **pinned group** — orbit's look and test cards from every thread, drawn together at the top or the bottom by his settings row
- **Tab loop** — the one ring Tab and shift-Tab walk across our mods (`_shared/tab-loop.js`): each mod's **Tab stops** (the quill, orbit's steer boxes) in the order the layout places the mods, never herdr's own hidden stops
- **Tab member** — a mod's seat in the Tab loop: it publishes its Tab stops, asks for the next stop, and focuses its own when asked, or when its mod reloads under the keyboard; a mod joins the loop by making one, with no list to edit
- **code block** — a fenced block in a reply, drawn with a ⧉ copy button that copies its text
- **chip** — inline code in the quill drawn as one monospace token; the caret skips it, one delete removes it
- **land** — copy a mod from frame into herdr's real mods folder (`pnpm herdr-mods:land`); the **watcher** lands each save that passes `herdr-app check`
- **zoo** — the third-party setup our chat, layout and clones began from; taken over on 2026-11-12, its mods set aside
